# Data Tasks

Managed one-time jobs for large-scale data work. Useful when you need to enrich a catalogue, run cross-referencing across thousands of parts, or map fitments in bulk — without building a pipeline yourself.

**Submit a task:** [plan.parts/portal/tasks/create](https://plan.parts/portal/tasks/create)

---

## Task Types

### Categories
- **Enrichment** — add missing attributes, descriptions, or categories to your parts list
- **Cross-referencing** — map your article numbers to OEM / IAM analogs at scale
- **Fitment mapping** — link parts to compatible vehicles (K-Type / brand/model/year)
- **File analysis** — parse and structure a supplier price list or catalogue file
- **Database queries** — custom lookups against the plan.parts dataset
- **Custom** — any other structured data task; describe your requirements

---

## Pricing

| Complexity | Token Cost |
|------------|-----------|
| Simple | 100 – 500 tokens |
| Medium | 500 – 2,000 tokens |
| Complex | 2,000 – 5,000+ tokens |

Tokens are charged **after** the task is completed and you approve the result.

---

## How It Works

1. Submit a task at [plan.parts/portal/tasks/create](https://plan.parts/portal/tasks/create) — describe what data you have and what result you need.
2. Receive a cost estimate before work begins.
3. Approve → task is processed.
4. Download results; tokens are deducted.
